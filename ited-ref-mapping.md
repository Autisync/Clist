# ITED / REF → FieldReady data model

Research note, 17 August 2026. Sources listed at the end.

Confidence is marked throughout: **[confirmed]** traces to a source I read in full,
**[partial]** comes from a table of contents or a secondary summary, **[open]** is
something I could not verify and you need to check before building to it.

---

## 1. Bottom line

The after-action report should not be something we invent. Portuguese installers are
already legally obliged to produce a **Relatório de Ensaios e Funcionalidade (REF)** and
a **termo de responsabilidade de execução** for every ITED installation, on a ten-working-day
clock, with defined contents. FieldReady already collects most of the REF's raw material
as a side effect of the technician doing his job.

That reframes the product. "Checklist app" is a nice-to-have that competes with Jobber.
"The thing that produces your REF correctly and on time" is a compliance tool, which is
stickier, easier to price, and much harder for a generic FSM vendor to copy.

Three findings below change the design as it currently stands. One of them contradicts a
decision we made last session.

## 2. What the regulation actually requires

**Procedimento de Avaliação das ITED, Edição 2024** — approved 26 July 2024, in force
1 August 2024. **[confirmed]**

The procedure has four phases:

1. Elaboração do REF
2. Emissão do termo de responsabilidade de execução
3. Submissão do REF à ANACOM
4. Colocação do rótulo ITED (optional)

### 2.1 The REF is composed of five documents [confirmed]

| | Content |
|---|---|
| a | **Ficha de identificações**, per the annex to the procedure |
| b | **Results of the tests** performed per the applicable Manual ITED, stating the date they were carried out and identifying the technician who carried them out |
| c | **Copies of the calibration certificates** of the measuring and test equipment used |
| d | **Copy of the executed project** |
| e | Any additional documentation the installer considers relevant (e.g. council opinions) |

Four notes attach to this **[confirmed]**:

1. The `ID do REF` on the ficha de identificações must match the REF identification field
   inside the corresponding termo de responsabilidade de execução.
2. If page one of the ficha is too small, page two is free text.
3. **Wherever possible, test results should be those generated by the test equipment's own
   software** — numeric or graphical — provided they demonstrate conformity with the
   applicable Manual ITED.
4. If the installer cannot perform the tests (e.g. lacks the equipment) he may contract
   another entity, which must be identified in the test records.

### 2.2 Termo de responsabilidade de execução [confirmed]

- Issued **exclusively** in ANACOM's reserved ITED-ITUR area, and automatically submitted
  to ANACOM from there. We cannot replace this — at most we prepare the data for it.
- Must reach ANACOM, the dono da obra, the diretor da obra, the diretor de fiscalização
  da obra, and the building owner/administration **within 10 working days of installation
  completion**.
- Copies for the other parties are obtained from the reserved area.
- A **paper copy must be placed inside the ATE**, protected. In single-dwelling buildings
  it goes inside the ATI.

### 2.3 REF submission [confirmed]

- By email only, to **ref@anacom.pt**.
- **Within 10 working days of the date the termo de responsabilidade was issued.**
- Email subject must **begin** with the termo de responsabilidade number, then whatever
  the technician wants.
- PDF, one or more files. Zip permitted. File-transfer platforms are not accepted.

### 2.4 Tests themselves — Manual ITED 4.ª edição

Approved 12 March 2020, in force 1 April 2020. **[partial]** — these dates come from
search summaries of ANACOM and ISQ pages, not from a document I read end to end. The
ATEC copy of the manual is consistent with them but does not state them on a page I
retrieved.

Chapter 6 (Ensaios) covers three separate network families, each with its own mandatory
test set **[partial — from the manual's table of contents]**:

| Network | Section | Mandatory-test table |
|---|---|---|
| Pares de cobre (PC) | 6.1 | Tabela 6.1 |
| Cabos coaxiais — rede coletiva e individual | 6.2.1 | Tabela 6.4 |
| Cabos coaxiais — sistemas S/MATV | 6.2.2 | Tabela 6.5 |
| Fibra ótica (FO) | 6.3 | Tabela 6.14 |
| Equipamentos de ensaio e medida | 6.4 | Tabela 6.21 |

The limit-value tables are **Tabela 6.7** and **6.9** (atenuação e slope), **Tabela 6.12**
(nível de sinal e MER) and **Tabela 6.17** (atenuação em FO), on pages 165–172. I could
not extract the numeric contents — see open questions.

## 3. What this changes in FieldReady

### 3.1 The calibration loop — best new idea to come out of this

REF item (c) requires calibration certificates for the equipment used. Our readiness
checklist already carries "Medidor de campo DVB-T2 (calibrado)" as a tick box. It should
not be a tick box. It should be an equipment record with a calibration certificate file
and an expiry date, and **the readiness gate should refuse to dispatch when the
calibration has expired** — because measurements taken on an out-of-calibration meter
produce a REF that is not defensible.

That closes a loop nothing else in the market closes: a pre-job gate whose justification
is a post-job legal document.

### 3.2 Instrument export beats photo-OCR — this reverses last session's decision

Note 3 says test results should preferably be **generated by the test equipment's own
software**. A phone photo of the meter's screen, transcribed by OCR, is a human-mediated
copy — weaker evidence than the instrument's own export, and arguably not what the
procedure asks for.

Revised priority for ITED work:

1. **Import from the meter** (USB/Bluetooth/file export) — the compliant path
2. Photo of the meter screen with OCR — acceptable fallback, keeps the no-typing promise
3. Manual entry — always available

The phone UX we built stays exactly as it is for non-ITED work (a one-off antenna at an
existing house). For ITED jobs, "Fotografar o medidor" should drop below "Importar do
medidor". This is worth checking against the specific meters your installers own before
committing — instrument export is only the compliant path if their meters actually support it.

### 3.3 We currently model one network out of three

The prototype tests DVB-T2 coax only. A real ITED building job requires copper-pair,
coax and fibre test sets, each with its own methods and limits. Scoping to coax/S-MATV
first is defensible — it is your vertical — but the schema must not assume coax.

### 3.4 The deadlines are the product

Two hard, countable clocks: termo within 10 working days of completion, REF within 10
working days of the termo. Both in Portuguese working days, so a holiday calendar is
required. An app that simply never lets these lapse is worth money on its own, and it is
about a week of work.

### 3.5 Multiple manual editions coexist

The 2024 procedure refers to "o Manual ITED aplicável" **[confirmed]**, and the previous
evaluation procedure was titled for ITED1, ITED2 and ITED3 **[partial — from a page title
only]**. Which edition governs appears to depend on the
building's project, not on today's date. `manual_edition` belongs on the job.

## 7A. Follow-up research, 18 August — DL 123/2009 primary text

The three questions left open in §6 (v1) were pushed further. One is now resolved with
primary-source confidence; two hit a genuine, repeated technical wall and are closed out
with a pragmatic default instead — documented here so the reasoning is auditable.

### 7A.1 ITED applicability boundary — resolved **[confirmed, primary source]**

Read in full: Decreto-Lei n.º 123/2009 (updated version), Artigos 58.º–89.º, via
`pgdlisboa.pt`. ANACOM's own site would not serve this text to automated fetching, but
`pgdlisboa.pt` did — one working method was deep-linking a specific article
(`?artigo_id=1456A00NN`), which returns a readable ~11-article window instead of the
whole diploma, sidestepping a length cap that had silently truncated two earlier fetches
(this Manual ITED body and DL 123/2009 itself, both cut off mid-document on the first try).

Four articles settle the question:

- **Artigo 60.º** — buildings are exempt from the whole ITED chapter only if, "by their
  nature and specific purpose," they present a "remote probability" of ever needing
  electronic communications infrastructure — a narrow carve-out (sheds, pure industrial
  structures), not a general existing-building exemption.
- **Artigo 71.º / 72.º** — draws the line I was originally looking for, but not where I
  expected it. It is not "new build vs repair." It is whether the work sits inside a
  municipal licensing/prior-communication process (Artigo 71.º → full formal project
  regime) or outside one (Artigo 72.º → the technical project is simplified and kept
  privately by the owner, produced only on inspection — the obligation doesn't disappear,
  it just isn't filed with the município).
- **Artigo 73.º §1** — "A instalação, **a alteração e a conservação** das ITED devem ser
  efetuadas por instalador habilitado." Installation, *alteration*, and *conservation*
  all require a qualified installer — not new installation alone.
- **Artigo 83.º** (Secção VI, "ITED dos edifícios construídos" — ITED of already-built
  buildings) — this is the operative article for TDT.pt's actual work: *"A alteração das
  infraestruturas de telecomunicações, nomeadamente para a instalação de fibra ótica,
  deve ser precedida de projeto técnico simplificado... termos de responsabilidade [do
  projetista e do instalador]... à ANACOM, no prazo de 10 dias."* Fibre is given as the
  example ("nomeadamente") — the article is not limited to it. Altering telecom
  infrastructure in an existing building carries a simplified-but-real project and dual
  termo obligation, enforced: Artigo 89.º §3(x) makes breaching Artigo 83.º a
  contraordenação classified "muito grave," fines €500–1,000,000 depending on offender
  size.

**What this means for a residential antenna job.** The statute regulates
*infraestruturas* — conduit, cabling, distribution frames, outlets — not the terminal
antenna hardware in isolation. So the honest boundary is:

| Scenario | Regime |
|---|---|
| New build or work requiring a município licence/prior communication | Artigo 71.º — full formal ITED project, in scope |
| Existing building, work alters the shared coax/cabling infrastructure (new run, new outlet, re-cabling the S/MATV head-end) | Artigo 83.º — simplified project + dual termo, in scope |
| Existing building, narrow like-for-like swap of the antenna/LNB head with no cabling or outlet work | Genuinely ambiguous on the statute text alone |

That third row is a real edge case, not a gap in this research — it turns on facts about
each specific job that no amount of desk reading resolves. **Product decision:** default
every job that touches shared building coax to Artigo 83.º scope (`ited_full`
obligations apply); expose a technician-facing flag for the narrow head-swap case,
routed to office review rather than decided by the app. Given the fine schedule above,
the safer default is the one that over-includes.

### 7A.2 Ficha de identificações fields — RESOLVED, 18 August, from the real document

Rex retrieved both `ProcedimentoAvaliacao_ITED_Edicao2024.pdf` (the official procedure,
including its annex) and `FichaIDProcedimentoAvaliacaoITEDedicao2024.docx` directly —
ANACOM's site never yielded either to automated fetching (see the original note below,
kept for the record), so this needed a human with a browser. The PDF's annex (pages 6–8)
is the authoritative ficha de identificações. Full field list, page 1:

- **Header:** `ID do REF`, `DATA` (dia/mês/ano)
- **INSTALADOR:** `NOME`, `N.º (ANACOM, OE, OET)` — the installer's professional
  registration number, not previously modelled anywhere in the schema (added, see §5).
- **EDIFÍCIO:** `MORADA`, `CÓDIGO POSTAL`, `LOCALIDADE POSTAL`, `MANUAL ITED APLICÁVEL`
- **CABOS INSTALADOS** (table, one row each): Pares de cobre / Coaxial / Fibra ótica ×
  columns `Marca e Modelo/Referência`, `Classe RPC` (the Regulamento dos Produtos de
  Construção fire-reaction class referenced in Manual ITED §2.3)
- **OUTROS MATERIAIS E DISPOSITIVOS INSTALADOS** (table, `Designação` × `Marca e
  Modelo/Referência`): Tomadas; RC-PC/RC-CC/RC-FO; RG-PC/RG-CC/RG-FO; TDT (Antenas, DST
  e cabeça de rede); Tampa da CVM (com indicação da classe EN124); CAM; Outros
- **DOCUMENTAÇÃO OBRIGATÓRIA** (checklist, confirms REF items b/c/d from §2.1 above):
  test results with date and technician; calibration certificates; executed project copy
- **DOCUMENTAÇÃO FACULTATIVA:** free text field for other documents the installer
  considers relevant

Page 2, exactly as note 2 in §2.1 promised: **OUTRAS IDENTIFICAÇÕES RELEVANTES** — a
single open free-text page, used when page 1 doesn't have room.

**Applied:** `ref_document.ficha_fields` in `03-schema.sql` now documents this exact
shape instead of a guessed one, and `app_user` gained a `professional_registration_number`
column — the "N.º (ANACOM, OE, OET)" field has no home anywhere else in the schema and
every ITED job's REF needs it. Both changes re-verified against `verify-schema.mjs`.

### 7A.3 Manual ITED numeric limits — RESOLVED, 18 August, from the real document

Same story as §7A.2: automated fetching hit a wall (ANACOM's original PDF returned
empty; the ATEC mirror truncated before chapter 6), and Rex closed it by retrieving both
`Manual-ITED4-vfinal-atec.pdf` and `ManualITED4edicao_2019.pdf` directly and having me
read pages 165–176. Real tables, in full, below — cited by table number and page so
every value here traces back to source.

**One structural correction to the original placeholder design:** ITED tests **two
different points**, not one five-parameter test. The prototype's `TDT_TESTS` (nível de
sinal, MER, CBER, LBER, C/N all in one grid, measured "per tomada") conflated them.
The real structure:

- **At the TT (tomada — the outlet a technician actually measures at, per job)** —
  Tabela 6.12, two parameters only: nível de sinal and MER.
- **At the entrada da CR (head-end input — a different, upstream test point, not
  per-outlet)** — Tabela 6.13, four parameters: nível de sinal, SNR, CBER, and (satellite
  only) PER.

For FieldReady's phone-based per-outlet AAR flow, **Tabela 6.12 is the one that matters**
— it's what a technician standing at a tomada with a field meter actually checks. Tabela
6.13 belongs to head-end commissioning, a different job step, out of scope for the
per-outlet grid the prototype already built. `job_test_result.location_label` and
`network_type` already accommodate both if a future job type needs 6.13 too.

#### Tabela 6.12 — Valores limite de nível de sinal e MER (p.170) — the TT/tomada test

| Sistema instalado | Modulação | Nível de sinal — recomendado | Nível de sinal — limites | MER — recomendado | MER — limite inferior |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TDT via hertziana (Zona digital A – DVB-T) | 64 QAM | 55 dBµV | 45–74 dBµV | 26 dB | 19,5 dB |
| TDT via satélite (Zona digital B – DVB-S2) | 8 PSK | 55 dBµV | 47–77 dBµV | 17 dB | 14 dB |

Note in the manual: these values derive from EN 60728-1 for the FEC/modulation
combinations used on the Portuguese TDT platform; other systems need their own norm.

#### Tabela 6.13 — Valores medidos à entrada da CR (p.171) — head-end, not per-outlet

| Sistema | Modulação | Parâmetro | Valor limite |
|---|---|---|---|
| TDT via hertziana (Zona A – DVB-T) | 64 QAM | Nível de sinal | 31,6 dBµV |
| | | SNR | 20 dB |
| | | CBER | 2×10⁻⁴ |
| TDT via satélite (Zona B – DVB-S2) | 8 PSK | Nível de sinal | 44 dBµV |
| | | SNR | 13,9 dB |
| | | CBER | 2×10⁻⁴ |
| | | PER | 1×10⁻⁷ |

Measurement condition stated in the manual: 10 m of cable between antenna and CR.

#### Tabela 6.7 / 6.9 — Valores limite de atenuação e de slope, coax (pp.166–167)

| Rede | Faixa de frequência | Atenuação | Slope |

| Individual (6.9) | 950–2150 MHz | 23,4 dB | 8,4 dB |

Classe de ligação garantida: TCD-C-M, per Tabela 6.4/6.5's method (6.2.1).

| Comprimento de onda | Atenuação |
|---|---|
| 1310 nm | 1,8 dB |
| 1550 nm | 1,8 dB |
